## Title

## Short Description (80 chars)

## Full Description (4000 chars)
Musicya is a feature-rich offline music player for Android with a stunning Neo-Brutalist design. Play your local music collection with powerful organization tools and a beautiful interface.

KEY FEATURES:

🎵 SMART MUSIC PLAYER
- Play all your local audio files
- Fuzzy search that understands typos
- Smart playlists: Recently Added, Most Played, Favorites
- Queue management with drag-and-drop reordering
- Crossfade between tracks for seamless playback
- Sleep timer with preset durations

🚗 CAR MODE
- Extra-large controls for safe driving
- Simplified interface with big touch targets
- Easy playlist and queue access

🎛️ AUDIO ENHANCEMENT
- 10-band equalizer with presets: Pop, Rock, Jazz, Classical, Hip Hop, Electronic, R&B, Country, Vocal, Flat
- Custom EQ curves for audiophiles
- Playback speed control from 0.5x to 2.0x

📱 SYSTEM INTEGRATION
- Home screen widget with album art
- Lock screen media controls
- Quick Settings tile for notification shade
- App shortcuts for quick actions
- Deep link support for sharing

🎨 CUSTOMIZATION
- Light and dark theme support
- Custom primary colors
- Theme transition animations
- Material 3 design

📊 LIBRARY MANAGEMENT
- Browse by artist, album, genre, or folder
- Statistics screen with listening history
- Backup and restore settings
- Duplicate song detection
- Library import/export

🔒 PRIVACY FOCUS
- 100% offline - no internet required for playback
- No tracking or analytics in core features
- No account required
- Your music stays on your device

## Privacy Policy
Musicya does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. All music playback is performed locally on your device. The optional analytics and crash reporting features are disabled by default and can be turned off in settings.
